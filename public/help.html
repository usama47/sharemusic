Setup & help

Keep everyone on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Start the host once with npm start and keep it running.

1. Listen together

Open the music page and tap Join and enable audio. The host chooses and starts the song. Your Pause / Resume button changes playback for everyone.

Turn up media volume. If audio is blocked, tap Join again; on the dashboard, tap Enable audio on this device. Bluetooth speakers can add delay.

Open music

2. Set up voice once

Each phone needs to trust this host’s certificate. Use the guided setup below to download it and follow the steps for your phone. Then open Voice, enter your name, tap Join voice and allow microphone access.

Open guided voice setup

The mic starts muted. Hold to talk, or turn on open mic for hands-free conversation. Enable sound if you cannot hear friends. Leaving releases the mic; hiding the page mutes it.

Opening voice leaves music playback on this device. It does not stop the room’s music.

Need a hand?

Microphone permission was denied

Open the browser’s site settings beside the address bar and allow Microphone. Also check microphone permission for the browser in your phone’s settings. Return to Voice and tap Join again.

A certificate warning appears

Return to guided voice setup and finish installing and trusting the certificate. Check that the phone’s date and time are correct. If the host changed Wi-Fi, restart its app and use its current address.

Connecting, buffering, or friends cannot hear me

Keep the host running and all phones on the same network. Check the mic indicator and your volume. Leave and rejoin voice. Guest Wi-Fi can block connections between phones; use a network that lets devices communicate.

Does this need internet? Will songs survive a restart?

After dependencies and certificates are set up, the app uses your local network. Keep the server running to listen or talk. Uploaded songs stay on the host across restarts; the current playback and voice session reset.
